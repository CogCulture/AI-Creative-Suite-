BILLING FAQ

Pricing Questions?

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime? +

Yes. You can cancel, downgrade, or upgrade your plan directly inside your account billing settings. Changes are pro-rated immediately.

What happens at the end of the free trial? +

At the end of your 14-day trial, you will be shifted to a view-only tier. You won't be charged unless you explicitly opt to upgrade to the Pro plan.

Do you offer discounts for annual plans? +

Yes. Annual billing plans offer a 20% discount compared to monthly billing cycles. Please toggle annual billing inside checkout.

What is a closed-loop security model? +

For Enterprise clients, we isolate all models, databases, and generation checkpoints. Your input prompts, logos, and output materials are never uploaded to the public internet or used to retrain our central weights.
