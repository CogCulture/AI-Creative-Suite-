Company Story

Cog Culture began in 2012 as a digital-first agency with a simple philosophy: work so closely with clients that we become an extension of their team.

Since then, we've evolved into a fully integrated 360° agency, expanding our capabilities across strategy, creativity, technology and AI. We've grown to a team of over 150, partnered with leading Indian and global brands, and earned recognition through prestigious national and international awards.

We've grown in every way imaginable, but the way we work hasn't changed. Our clients don't see us as an agency. They see us as an extension of their team.
